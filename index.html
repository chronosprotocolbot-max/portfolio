Project Case Studies

Select a project below to see what it does, why it matters and how the workflow keeps important decisions under human control.

HR Candidate Sifter

What it does

Collects CVs from Google Drive, prevents duplicate applications, extracts structured candidate data and evaluates each CV against a defined position.

Why it matters

Recruiters receive traceable scores, confidence information and recommendations in an Appsmith review dashboard instead of manually comparing every document from scratch.

Human control

The workflow supports recruiter review and communication preparation, but it does not make an automatic hiring decision.

Stack

n8n · Supabase · Appsmith · Google Drive · Gemini · Gmail

Prospect Finder

What it does

Searches Google Places across multiple query variants and result pages, checks usage limits, removes duplicates, enriches company records and evaluates evidence against configurable criteria.

Why it matters

Small businesses and agencies can build a qualified prospect list without repeating the same research manually.

Human control

Messages are reviewed and approved in Appsmith before approved content becomes a Gmail draft.

Stack

n8n · Appsmith · Supabase · Google Places · OpenAI · Gmail · optional RAG

AI Leasing CRM

What it does

Handles rental inquiries, matches requirements with properties, coordinates viewings, schedules follow-ups, requests documents and records won or lost outcomes.

Additional module

The Telegram assistant can recognise properties from complete or partial screenshots, combine context from additional images and support controlled landlord follow-up.

Human control

Communication and availability decisions remain with the operator while lead activity and status changes are recorded in Supabase.

Stack

n8n · Supabase · Appsmith · OpenAI · Gmail · Telegram · Google Calendar

BrightFlow Chatbot

What it does

Supports website chat, email, Telegram text and voice inputs, maintains conversation context, uses business-specific knowledge and synchronises confirmed appointments with Google Calendar.

Why it matters

The same core can be configured for clinics, agencies, consultants, property services and other appointment-based organisations.

Human control

Urgent or sensitive cases can be escalated to a person through email and Telegram alerts, with manual calendar controls built into the workflow.

Stack

n8n · Supabase · Appsmith · OpenAI · Vapi · Google Calendar · Gmail · Telegram

AI Models and Functions

Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Preview — HR Candidate Sifter

Used for job-advert extraction, CV evaluation and candidate-to-position matching. The workflow requests structured JSON containing scores, matched requirements, missing must-haves, red flags, recruiter summary, confidence and recommendation status.

GPT-5 mini — research, web evidence and vision

Used in Prospect Finder for evidence-based company research, permitted marketplace checks and structured enrichment packets, with controlled web-search calls. Used in the leasing Telegram assistant for visual property recognition from full or partial screenshots and comparison against candidate property images.

GPT-4.1 mini — offer and outreach generation

Used to turn validated company evidence into an offer, proposal and outreach draft. Separate validation logic checks JSON structure, unsupported claims, uncertainty, message length and approval requirements before a Gmail draft is created.

GPT-4o and GPT-4o mini — leasing and intake workflows

Used across rental inquiry intake, property matching, follow-ups, viewing management and decision workflows. These steps classify requests, extract structured requirements, prepare responses and support scheduling while leaving external communication and important decisions under human control.

Embeddings and RAG context

OpenAI embeddings and vector retrieval were used where a workflow needed controlled knowledge-base context. Retrieved evidence was attached to the model input rather than treated as an automatic decision, and RAG could be enabled or disabled per campaign.
